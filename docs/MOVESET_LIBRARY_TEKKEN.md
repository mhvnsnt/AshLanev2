# Moveset Library — TEKKEN

**Date:** 2026-10-06
**Purpose:** Per-character combat reference for AshLane fighter design. When we build a fighter, we pull from this.
**How to read:** Tekken notation — `1` left punch, `2` right punch, `3` left kick, `4` right kick; `f/b/u/d` directions; `WS` while standing, `FC` full crouch, `WR` while running, `CH` counter hit, `CD` crouch dash (`f,n,d,d/f`).
**Systems reference:** `COMBAT_TEARDOWN.md`.

## MISHIMA FAMILY & KARATE

### Kazuya Mishima
- **Style:** Mishima-ryu karate.
- **Archetype:** 50/50 mixup monster / punish turtle.
- **Signature moves:** Electric Wind God Fist (`f,n,d,d/f+2`, just-frame electric launcher — the execution skill-check); hellsweep (`CD+4,4` — low/low knockdown); twin pistons (`WS+1,2`); demon's paw (`f+2`); spinning demon (`f+4,4,4,4`).
- **Grapples/throws:** Tombstone (`d/b+1+2`); strong generic game; no chain throws — his threat is the 50/50, not the grab.
- **Key combos:** EWGF → `f+4` → `b+2,4,1` → screw → ender; hellsweep → `WS+4,4` pickup.
- **Stances:** Crouch dash / mist step is his stance — everything flows from it. Devil form adds laser/beam tools.
- **Movement identity:** THE wavedash character. Wavedash pressure (hellsweep vs EWGF mixup) defines his offense.
- **What makes them distinct:** The purest 50/50 character in fighting games. Every approach asks: block low (eat the electric launcher) or stand (eat the hellsweep). Punishment is elite — 13f WS launcher, best-in-class.
- **Best tools:** EWGF, hellsweep, `d/f+2` (safe mid launcher-ish), `b+1+2` sabaki-ish options.
- **AshLane pull:** The wavedash 50/50 loop — crouch-dash stance that branches low-sweep vs mid-launcher. Give ONE AshLane bruiser this exact loop.

### Jin Kazama
- **Style:** Mishima-ryu karate blended with Kazama tradition (T8: more Kazama-flavored).
- **Archetype:** Balanced / poke-turtle with explosive punishment.
- **Signature moves:** Electric Wind Hook Fist (EWHF — his EWGF); `d/f+1,4`; `b+2,1`; hellsweep (`CD+4,3`); Zenshin stance (`d+1+2`) with low/mid options.
- **Grapples/throws:** Command throw `qcf+1+2` (unbreakable-ish threat); solid generics.
- **Key combos:** EWHF → `b+3~f,ZEN 1+2` style routes; `d/f+2` launcher → staple juggle.
- **Stances:** Zenshin (power stance with advancing options); crouch dash like all Mishimas.
- **Movement identity:** Wavedash-capable but more poke-oriented than Kazuya; strong keepout (`d+3+3`, `b+2,1`).
- **What makes them distinct:** The "honest Mishima" — less degenerate 50/50 than Kazuya, better pokes and whiff punishment, Zenshin gives him a pressure stance Kazuya lacks.
- **Best tools:** EWHF, `d/f+1` pokes, Zenshin mixups, `2,4` CH confirm.
- **AshLane pull:** Zenshin-style power stance — a mid-string stance that advances and branches. Good template for a "disciplined martial artist" AshLane fighter.

### Devil Jin
- **Style:** Mishima-ryu + devil powers (lasers, flight).
- **Archetype:** Mishima 50/50 + gimmick zoning.
- **Signature moves:** EWGF (electric); hellsweep; `u/f+4` (laser cannon spike); `b+2,3` beam; `f+3+4` flying cross chop; `d+3+4` low laser.
- **Grapples/throws:** Standard Mishima set; nothing chain-like.
- **Key combos:** EWGF → laser screw routes; `d/f+2` → standard juggle with laser ender.
- **Stances:** Crouch dash; Mourning Crow flight stance (`u+3+4`); laser stances.
- **Movement identity:** Wavedash + flight — unique vertical dimension.
- **What makes them distinct:** Mishima fundamentals grafted onto a flying laser demon. The most "anime" Mishima — keepout lasers, flight mixups, huge damage.
- **Best tools:** EWGF, hellsweep, laser keepout, `d/b+2` poke.
- **AshLane pull:** Flight stance as a limited resource (meter-gated hover with 2-3 attack options). Only for supernatural AshLane characters — scarcity is the point.

### Heihachi Mishima
- **Style:** Mishima-ryu karate (the founder's brutal variant).
- **Archetype:** Aggressive turtle / CH bully.
- **Signature moves:** EWGF; hellsweep; `f+1+2` (powerful mid, wall splat); `d/b+2` (low poke); `qcf+2` demon uppercut; `b+2` (great CH tool); headbutt (`WS+2`).
- **Grapples/throws:** Ultimate tackle (`d+1+2` — tackle into punch/kick/throw mixup); strong generics.
- **Key combos:** EWGF → `f+4` → staples; `qcf+2` CH → big damage.
- **Stances:** Crouch dash; Raijin stance (T8 — thunder stance with options).
- **Movement identity:** Wavedash; older, slower walk but oppressive buttons.
- **What makes them distinct:** The bully Mishima — less about the pure 50/50, more about CH fishing and frame-trapping you to death. The tackle is a mini-grapple game of its own.
- **Best tools:** `f+1+2`, `b+2`, EWGF, ultimate tackle, `d/f+1` pressure.
- **AshLane pull:** The tackle — a running grab that becomes its own strike/throw mixup minigame. Perfect for an AshLane enforcer archetype.

### Reina Mishima
- **Style:** Mishima-ryu + Taido (Heihachi's successor).
- **Archetype:** Stance-dance Mishima / trickster.
- **Signature moves:** EWGF (electric); hellsweep; Heaven's Wrath stance (`b+3+4`) with multiple options; Wind God Step mixups.
- **Grapples/throws:** Mishima-standard set.
- **Key combos:** EWGF → Heaven's Wrath routes; standard Mishima juggles with stance extensions.
- **Stances:** Heaven's Wrath (stance with low/mid/throw branches); Sentai; crouch dash.
- **Movement identity:** Wavedash + stance trickery — the most stance-heavy Mishima.
- **What makes them distinct:** Mishima 50/50 engine with a stance-dancer's soul. Heaven's Wrath gives her Xiaoyu-style unpredictability inside the Mishima shell.
- **Best tools:** EWGF, hellsweep, Heaven's Wrath mixups, `d/f+2`.
- **AshLane pull:** Stance-branching inside a 50/50 character — the "what stance did she just enter" guessing game. Template for deceptive-speedster AshLane fighters.

### Jun Kazama
- **Style:** Kazama-style traditional martial arts (defensive, flowing).
- **Archetype:** Defensive / punish / keepout (T8 return).
- **Signature moves:** `1,2,2` strings; `d/f+2` launcher; `b+4` keepout; `u/f+3` hopkick-ish; Izumo stance flows.
- **Grapples/throws:** Standard set; emphasis on defensive throws.
- **Key combos:** `d/f+2` → staple; CH `b+4` → followups.
- **Stances:** Izumo (flowing stance with evasion and branches).
- **Movement identity:** Strong backdash, keepout pokes, whiff punishment over aggression.
- **What makes them distinct:** The anti-Mishima Mishima-adjacent — where Kazuya attacks, Jun defends and punishes. Flowing, "aikido-like" defensive answers in a rushdown family.
- **Best tools:** `b+4`, `d/f+2`, `1,2` hit-confirms, Izumo evasion.
- **AshLane pull:** The defensive-counter archetype done right — a fighter whose whole kit is "punish your mistake." AshLane needs this foil to its rushdown cast.

### Paul Phoenix
- **Style:** Judo + Kyokushin hybrid (self-taught brawler).
- **Archetype:** 50/50 / deathfist gambles.
- **Signature moves:** Deathfist (`qcf+2` — the iconic shoulder charge, huge damage, unsafe); Demolition Man (`d+4,2,1+2` — low/mid/mid string); `d/f+2` launcher; `b+1+2` shoulder; `qcb+2` sway launcher.
- **Grapples/throws:** Backbreaker (`qcb+1+2`); strong Judo-flavored command throws.
- **Key combos:** `d/f+2` → `b+4` → Demolition Man ender; CH `qcb+2` → deathfist.
- **Stances:** Sway (`qcb` — ducking sway with launcher/throw options).
- **Movement identity:** Explosive forward bursts; deathfist as a round-ender.
- **What makes them distinct:** The coin-flip character — every round can end on one Deathfist read. Highest damage-per-read in Tekken, paid for with unsafety.
- **Best tools:** Deathfist, Demolition Man, `d/f+2`, sway mixups.
- **AshLane pull:** The "one big read" bruiser — a fighter built around a single terrifying unsafe launcher. (Note: Paul appears in Urban Reign too — see UR doc for the 4-button adaptation.)

### Marshall Law
- **Style:** Jeet Kune Do (Bruce Lee homage).
- **Archetype:** Rushdown / string pressure / CH.
- **Signature moves:** Dragon tail (`d/b+4` — low sweep); `d/f+2` launcher; `b+2,2` nunchaku-ish strings; `f+2+3` dragon charge; `WS+2` launcher; Junkyard (`b+2,3,4`).
- **Grapples/throws:** Standard set, nothing fancy — his game is strikes.
- **Key combos:** `d/f+2` → `4,u+3` → `f+2+3` style routes; CH dragon hammer → followups.
- **Stances:** Dragon stance (slide/approach options).
- **Movement identity:** Relentless forward pressure; slide mixups (`d/b+3+4`).
- **What makes them distinct:** The pressure-cooker — endless safe-ish strings that force you to guess low/mid under a barrage. Best 1-inch-punch flavor in the series.
- **Best tools:** Dragon tail, `d/f+1` pokes, Junkyard strings, slide mixups.
- **AshLane pull:** String-pressure design — long natural strings with hidden low/mid branches. Template for AshLane's martial-arts rushdown fighters. (Also in Urban Reign — see UR doc.)

## GRAPPLERS & HEAVY BODIES

### King II
- **Style:** Pro wrestling (Mexican lucha + catch).
- **Archetype:** THE grappler — throw mixup / oki.
- **Signature moves:** `f+2+3` (running powerbomb stance); `d/f+2,1` (mid/mid); `b+1` (great keepout); `u/f+3` hopkick; Jaguar step (`3+4`) with multiple branches.
- **Grapples/throws:** ~48 throws. Generic `1+3`/`2+4`; command throws: Giant Swing (`qcf+1` — 1-break, huge damage, ring-out style carry), Muscle Buster (`d/b,f+2+4` — 2-break), Tombstone (`WS+2+4`); **multi-throw chains**: Rolling Death Cradle starter (`d/b+1+3` chains), Cobra Clutch, Reverse Special Stretch Bomb — each link breakable by `1`/`2`/`1+2` read off the lead arm, ending in unbreakable finishers. Jaguar step has unblockable tackle options.
- **Key combos:** `d/f+2,1` → `b+4` → screw → Muscle Buster ender (throw enders in combos); hopkick → staple.
- **Stances:** Jaguar step (run-up with throw/strike branches); crouch dash.
- **Movement identity:** Mediocre neutral movement; gets in with Jaguar step and wavedash-lite; once he's in throw range, the game changes.
- **What makes them distinct:** The throw-knowledge-check character. Fighting King is a quiz: know 10+ break inputs or die. Chain throws are Tekken's most dramatic moments — a 5-link chain does 70+ damage and the crowd goes wild.
- **Best tools:** Giant Swing, Muscle Buster, Jaguar step mixups, `b+1` keepout, chain-throw starters.
- **AshLane pull:** The multi-throw chain as a 2-link simplification — starter grab → branch A/B → unbreakable slam. Our grapple-system.ts already does paired playback; add the break-read minigame. King is the single most important Tekken reference for AshLane's wrestling-canon fighters (El Toro, etc.).

### Armor King (legacy — Tekken 7)
- **Style:** Pro wrestling (darker, strike-heavier than King).
- **Archetype:** Grappler / whiff-punish.
- **Signature moves:** `f+2,1` (dark upper); `d+3+4` (stomp); `b+1+2` (shoulder); wavedash mixups like a Mishima-lite.
- **Grapples/throws:** Tombstone piledriver; sharpshooter submission; chain throws (shorter chains than King but harder-hitting); Burning Knuckle throw.
- **Key combos:** `d/f+2` → staples; wavedash hellsweep-lite → followups.
- **Stances:** Crouch dash; wavedash.
- **Movement identity:** Better movement than King — a grappler who can actually play neutral like a Mishima.
- **What makes them distinct:** King if King had Mishima movement. The "complete" grappler — throws AND wavedash 50/50.
- **Best tools:** Tombstone, wavedash mixups, `f+2,1`, sharpshooter.
- **AshLane pull:** Grappler + wavedash hybrid — the template for an AshLane boss who is both a throw threat and a neutral threat.

### Craig Marduk (legacy)
- **Style:** Vale Tudo (MMA).
- **Archetype:** Big-body grappler / oki monster.
- **Signature moves:** `d+1+2` (tackle — mount position minigame); `f+3+4` (running spear); `d/b+3` (low); `u/f+3+4` (flying attack).
- **Grapples/throws:** Tackle → mount → punch/throw/escape-guess minigame (his signature); air throws; ground-and-pound.
- **Key combos:** Launchers into tackle oki; `d/f+1` → tackle setups.
- **Stances:** Vale Tudo stance (grounded options).
- **Movement identity:** Huge, slow, terrifying up close; tackle closes distance.
- **What makes them distinct:** The MMA grappler — his tackle-mount game is a whole subsystem: once mounted, the defender guesses punch/throw/getup while Marduk guesses the counter.
- **Best tools:** Tackle mount, `f+3+4` spear, ground throws, `d+1+2`.
- **AshLane pull:** The tackle-to-mount minigame — a grab that becomes a sustained positional guessing game. Great for AshLane's ground-game depth.

### Jack-8
- **Style:** Military cyborg brawling.
- **Archetype:** Big-body turtle / keepout / punish.
- **Signature moves:** `d/f+2` (mid launcher); `b+1+2` (shoulder charge); `d+1+2` (low crush); `f+1+2` (long-range poke); Gamma Howl stance (`d+1+2` hold).
- **Grapples/throws:** High-damage command throws; nothing chain-like — his throws are single big hits.
- **Key combos:** `d/f+2` → `f+3,1` → screw → ender; punish combos are his best damage.
- **Stances:** Gamma Howl (charged stance); crouch dash variants.
- **Movement identity:** Terrible sidestep (huge body), amazing backdash; plays at range with long limbs.
- **What makes them distinct:** The wall — out-ranges everyone, punishes everything, can't be stepped easily because his buttons are so long. Weakness: linearity and size (eats everything).
- **Best tools:** `d/f+2`, `b+1+2`, `f+1+2` keepout, punishment.
- **AshLane pull:** Big-body keepout design — long-range pokes + elite punishment, weak lateral movement. Template for AshLane's heavy enforcer types.

### Kuma II / Panda
- **Style:** "Bear" — mauling, swipes, salmon.
- **Archetype:** Joke-grappler / big-body gimmick.
- **Signature moves:** `d/f+1+2` (bear swipe); `b+1` (swipe keepout); `HBS` (Hunting Bear Stance) with roll/maul options; salmon recovery (heals!).
- **Grapples/throws:** Bear hug throws; rolling maul; surprisingly strong throw game for a joke character.
- **Key combos:** `d/f+2` → bear staples; HBS roll combos.
- **Stances:** Hunting Bear Stance (all-fours stance with unique moves); salmon.
- **Movement identity:** Huge, deceptively fast wavedash (Kuma can wavedash!), strong oki.
- **What makes them distinct:** The joke that kills you — top players win tournaments with the bear. HBS gives him a stance-dancer's options on a grappler's body.
- **Best tools:** HBS mixups, wavedash, `b+1`, bear hug throws.
- **AshLane pull:** Gimmick-done-right — a stance (all-fours maul stance) that completely changes a big body's gameplan. Permission for AshLane to get weird with stances.

### Bryan Fury
- **Style:** Kickboxing (cyborg brawler).
- **Archetype:** Counter-hit bully / keepout.
- **Signature moves:** `b+1` (the orbital heel — iconic keepout); `f+2,1` (mach punch); `qcb+3` (snake edge — huge low launcher, very unsafe); `d/f+2,3` (taunt setups); `1+2+3+4` taunt (the disrespect button, cancels into unblockable jet upper).
- **Grapples/throws:** Standard set; his game is CH, not throws.
- **Key combos:** `u/f+4` → `b+2,1` → screw → taunt jet-upper (the swaggiest combo in Tekken); CH `b+1` → followups.
- **Stances:** Taunt stance (cancellable, the mind-game engine).
- **Movement identity:** Best keepout in the game (`b+1`, `3+4`); walks you down with safe mids.
- **What makes them distinct:** The CH fisherman — every button is a trap. Taunt into jet upper is the most iconic combo in Tekken. Fights like a horror movie villain: slow advance, then sudden violence.
- **Best tools:** `b+1` orbital, mach punch, snake edge (the gamble), taunt, `d/f+1` pressure.
- **AshLane pull:** Taunt-as-mechanic — a cancellable taunt that baits and punishes. AshLane's personality-driven fighters (Static!) need exactly this: disrespect as a real tool.

### Fahkumram (Tekken 8 DLC)
- **Style:** Muay Thai (giant).
- **Archetype:** Big-body rushdown (unusual combination).
- **Signature moves:** `d/f+3` (knee); `b+1` (elbow keepout); `f+3+4` (charging knee); lightning stance options.
- **Grapples/throws:** Clinch throws; standard set.
- **Key combos:** Knee launcher → Muay Thai juggle routes.
- **Stances:** Lightning stance (charged knee/elbow branches).
- **Movement identity:** Huge but aggressive — breaks the "big = slow turtle" rule.
- **What makes them distinct:** A giant who rushes you down. Muay Thai clinch knees and elbows at a range nobody else has.
- **Best tools:** Knees, elbows, charging attacks, clinch.
- **AshLane pull:** Big-body rushdown — permission to make an AshLane heavy who attacks instead of turtling. (Also: Muay Thai knee/elbow/clinch kit reference for our Muay Thai style.)

## RUSHDOWN & TECHNIQUE

### Hwoarang
- **Style:** Taekwondo.
- **Archetype:** Stance-dance rushdown.
- **Signature moves:** `d+3,4` (low/high flamingo entry); `b+1` (keepout kick); `f+4,4,4,4` (spinning kicks); `d/f+3` (mid); backlash (`b+3` — the iconic turnaround kick).
- **Grapples/throws:** Standard set; air throws from flamingo.
- **Key combos:** `d/f+2` → `f+4,4` → screw → backlash ender; flamingo `3,4` CH routes.
- **Stances:** Right flamingo (`3~4`), left flamingo (`4~3`), left/right foot forward — each with its own movelist; stance transitions ARE his offense.
- **Movement identity:** Relentless; flamingo stances advance while attacking.
- **What makes them distinct:** The stance flowchart — you're never fighting Hwoarang, you're fighting his stance tree. Every blocked string branches into 3 more guesses.
- **Best tools:** Flamingo mixups, backlash, `b+1`, `d+3,4`.
- **AshLane pull:** Stance-tree offense — a fighter whose strings never end, they branch. Template for AshLane's most aggressive style (capoeira-adjacent).

### Steve Fox
- **Style:** Boxing (peek-a-boo).
- **Archetype:** Sway-stance rushdown — NO KICKS.
- **Signature moves:** `b+1` (albion hawk — lunging hook); `d/f+2` (uppercut launcher); `1,1,2` (sonic fang); `d+2+3` (duck); `b+3+4` (weave).
- **Grapples/throws:** Standard; his defense is the sway, not the grab.
- **Key combos:** `d/f+2` → `b+1` → screw → ender; CH albion → big damage.
- **Stances:** Duck (`d+2+3`), sway (`b+3+4`), peekaboo — weave stances that evade highs/mids and launch from evasion.
- **Movement identity:** Slips and weaves THROUGH your offense; the best defensive-offense in Tekken.
- **What makes them distinct:** A boxer with no kicks who beats kickboxers — because his stances make your highs whiff and his punish is a launcher. The purest "defense into offense" character.
- **Best tools:** Weave/duck stances, albion hawk, `d/f+2`, sonic fang.
- **AshLane pull:** Sway-stance system for AshLane's boxing style — duck/weave as real stances with launcher punishes, not just animations. Our boxers (Akon!) need this.

### Nina Williams
- **Style:** Assassination arts (Aikido + Koppojutsu).
- **Archetype:** String-pressure / chain-grab assassin.
- **Signature moves:** `d/f+3,2` (butterfly edge); `qcf+1` (wrist lock entry); `b+2,2` (strings); `d+3,2` (low/mid); evil mist (`qcf`).
- **Grapples/throws:** **Chain grabs**: `qcf+1` → multiple breakable branches (armbar sequences); command throws with 1/2/1+2 breaks; backhand slap.
- **Key combos:** `d/f+2` → staples; butterfly edge CH → followups.
- **Stances:** Evil mist (approach stance with throw/strike branches).
- **Movement identity:** Sidestep-heavy; evil mist closes distance with built-in mixup.
- **What makes them distinct:** The female grappler-assassin hybrid — strings that flow into chain grabs, so blocking the strikes gets you thrown and ducking the throws gets you launched.
- **Best tools:** Butterfly edge, chain grabs, `d/f+3,2`, evil mist.
- **AshLane pull:** Strike-into-chain-grab flow — strings that branch into throws. Perfect for AshLane's tricky fighters (Cass!).

### Anna Williams (Tekken 8 DLC)
- **Style:** Assassination arts (showier than Nina).
- **Archetype:** Stance-trickster / chaos.
- **Signature moves:** Chaos Judgement stance (`qcf+1+2` — stance with built-in throw reversal); `d+3+4` (low launcher); `b+3` (kick); rocket launcher (`qcf+2`).
- **Grapples/throws:** Chain grabs like Nina; Chaos Judgement reverses throws outright.
- **Key combos:** Standard launchers into Anna staples; CJ stance routes.
- **Stances:** Chaos Judgement (the stance that says "grab me and die").
- **Movement identity:** Unpredictable; CJ stance forces respect.
- **What makes them distinct:** Nina's chaos twin — where Nina is precise, Anna is a stance gambler. Chaos Judgement's built-in throw reversal is unique in Tekken.
- **Best tools:** Chaos Judgement, `d+3+4`, rocket launcher, chain grabs.
- **AshLane pull:** Stance with built-in throw reversal — a defensive stance that punishes grab attempts automatically. Great boss mechanic.

### Lee Chaolan
- **Style:** Jeet Kune Do / Mishima-ryu hybrid (the elegant one).
- **Archetype:** CH / poke / stance-trickster.
- **Signature moves:** `b+4` (the iconic CH kick); `d+3` (slide); `4,4,4,4` (rapid kicks); acid storm (`1+2`); `WS+2,3` (twin pistons-ish).
- **Grapples/throws:** Standard; nothing chain-like.
- **Key combos:** `d/f+2` → staples; CH `b+4` → `d+3` pickup → juggle.
- **Stances:** Hitman (`2+3` — sidestepping stance with options); mist step (Mishima-lite).
- **Movement identity:** Best backdash-and-poke game; slide mixups.
- **What makes them distinct:** The gentleman's rushdown — everything is a CH trap or a poke. The most "fundamentals" character: wins on spacing and punishment, not gimmicks.
- **Best tools:** `b+4`, slide, `d/f+1` pokes, Hitman stance.
- **AshLane pull:** CH-trap design philosophy — a fighter whose whole kit is "throw a safe poke, punish the whiff." AshLane's technical fighters need this discipline.

### Lars Alexandersson
- **Style:** Shorinji kempo / military.
- **Archetype:** Stance-rushdown / 50/50.
- **Signature moves:** `f+2,1` (strings); `d/b+2,1` (dynamic entry); `u/f+3` (orbital); Silent Entry (`d+1+2`) and Limited Entry stances; `f+1+2` (shoulder).
- **Grapples/throws:** Standard set.
- **Key combos:** `d/f+2` → `f+2,1` → screw → ender; stance-transition combos.
- **Stances:** Silent Entry / Limited Entry / Dynamic Entry — three entry stances, each with branches.
- **Movement identity:** Forward-marching pressure; entries advance with built-in mixups.
- **What makes them distinct:** The entry-stance flowchart — every approach is a stance with 3 options. T8's poster boy for "modern Tekken" design: aggressive, stance-heavy, Heat-friendly.
- **Best tools:** Entry stances, `f+2,1`, orbital, `d/b+2,1`.
- **AshLane pull:** Entry stances as approach tools — advancing stances with branches. Good for AshLane's military/corpo fighter types.

### Alisa Bosconovitch
- **Style:** Unique (android combat — chainsaws, rockets, detachable head).
- **Archetype:** Keepout / gimmick zoner.
- **Signature moves:** `d+3+4` (chainsaw stance); `b+3+4` (rockets); `f+2` (long poke); `d/b+2` (low); boot (`u/f+4`); Destructive Form (chainsaws out).
- **Grapples/throws:** Standard; her game is range.
- **Key combos:** `d/f+2` → staples; chainsaw pressure routes.
- **Stances:** Destructive Form (chainsaw stance — chip damage, different movelist); boot stance.
- **Movement identity:** Best keepout in the game alongside Jack; rockets and long limbs.
- **What makes them distinct:** The zoner in a rushdown game — wins by never letting you in. Chainsaws do chip; the head comes off (and explodes).
- **Best tools:** Chainsaws, rockets, `f+2`, boot, `d/b+2`.
- **AshLane pull:** Zoner archetype + chip-damage stance. AshLane's roster needs a keepout specialist; Alisa is the blueprint (adapt to street: long pipe/chain weapon fighter?).

### Ling Xiaoyu
- **Style:** Baguazhang / Piguaquan.
- **Archetype:** Evasive stance-dancer.
- **Signature moves:** `d/f+2` (launcher); `b+4,3` (strings); Phoenix stance (`3+4`); Art of Phoenix (`d+1+2` — the backturned-evade stance); `u/f+3` (hopkick).
- **Grapples/throws:** Standard; her evasion IS her defense.
- **Key combos:** `d/f+2` → staples; AOP `2` routes.
- **Stances:** Phoenix (low stance), Art of Phoenix (backturned, crushes highs, tiny hurtbox) — the most evasive stance in Tekken.
- **Movement identity:** Slippery — AOP ducks under mids, Phoenix crushes; hardest character to pin down.
- **What makes them distinct:** The untouchable — her stances shrink her hurtbox so much that half your movelist whiffs. Wins by making you miss, then launching the whiff.
- **Best tools:** AOP, Phoenix, `d/f+2`, `b+4,3`, backturned mixups.

### Eddy Gordo

- **Archetype:** Stance-dance / low-high chaos.

- **AshLane pull:** RLX-style grounded stance for AshLane's capoeira style (we have 16+ capoeira clips!). Fuego and our capoeira fighters need this.
