# AshLane Art Direction — Layered Themes (Owner 2026-10-05)

## Underlying Theme: Malakor + Shadow Wizard Money Gang
- Atmosphere, mood, worldbuilding
- Colors: pitch black × neon purple/blue/toxic green + gold
- Urban glamour, slightly menacing, slow and heavy
- Cultural mashup: street culture in dark fantasy framing
- Where it lives: lighting, fog, district atmosphere, Hollows, menu backgrounds, lore

## Overlying Theme: Tekken + Urban Reign
- Character presentation, combat feel, UI style
- Character cards: Tekken 8 / Urban Reign dramatic portraits
- Combat: Urban Reign multi-attacker brawling, Tekken move depth
- Menus: bold, high-energy, fighter-select energy
- Where it lives: character select, combat UI, move lists, HUD

## The Mix
Malakor is the world you walk through. Tekken/Urban Reign is how you fight in it.
Dark, moody streets → explosive, stylish combat.
