# GRAFFITI TEXTURE PIPELINE — real-world graffiti as proprietary game art

Owner directive (2026-10-08): AshLane's graffiti is PURELY research plus
images turned into textures. Real-world graffiti lives in the game, but as
proprietary SVG and PNG art — whatever format is best for graffiti in games —
created from reference, not copied.

## The pipeline

1. REFERENCE — collect real graffiti photos and video from:
   - FatCap and Street Art Cities (as visual reference and style research)
   - the Graffiti Hunter repo (~/workspace/graffiti-hunter), which builds
     its own photo/video/info archive over time
   - the owner's own street photography
2. STUDY — break down letterforms, color palettes, outlines, highlights,
   backgrounds, wall context. Research, not tracing.
3. CREATE — original SVG/PNG graffiti art in those real styles, drawn for
   the game. Proprietary, owned outright, consistent with AshLane's
   street-real art direction.
4. TEXTURE — convert to game-ready wall textures, decals, and tags at the
   right resolutions for the build.

## Rules

- Reference is fine. Direct 1:1 copies of someone's piece as a game texture
  are not — writers own their work even when the wall didn't ask for it.
  When a piece is iconic enough to want it exact, get the artist's
  permission or leave it out.
- Every texture in the game must be traceable: reference source on file,
  original art on file, no mystery assets.
- Style stays street-real and human — no AI-slop lettering. If a generated
  piece can't pass as a real writer's hand, it doesn't ship.
