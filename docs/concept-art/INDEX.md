## Visual Language Notes (from reference research)

**Urban Reign:** Grounded multi-attacker brawling, readable silhouettes, environmental storytelling in backgrounds. Lesson: stages must read instantly — the player should know where they are in one glance.

**Def Jam: Fight for NY:** Crowd as character, fashion as power, venues with personality (clubs, subway, junkyard). Lesson: every stage needs a distinct identity and crowd energy.

**Yakuza:** Kamurocho as a living character — neon-drenched, dense, every alley tells a story. Lesson: density over scale; detail in the small things (signs, vending machines, steam).

**Sleeping Dogs:** Hong Kong's verticality and wet-street reflections. Lesson: rain and reflections do heavy lifting for mood on a budget.

**Tekken:** Dramatic character presentation, bold color blocking, high-contrast key art. Lesson: combat moments need explosive, readable action framing.
